- October 24: [Controlled reset](../14_Saved/ForgeMCP_0.2.17_Landscape_Reset_Execution.md) returned the earlier experimental riverbed region to zero in the loaded Editor and documented later save evidence.
- October 24: [Basin, offset lobe, and west hill demonstration](../14_Saved/ForgeMCP_0.2.17_Lake_Basin_Hills_Demonstration.md) stopped after the west hill's `validation_failed_partial` result. [Latest terrain acceptance](../14_Saved/ForgeMCP_0.2.17_Terrain_Acceptance_Verification.md) passed live collision and package checks, while independent disk reload and close-up visual inspection remained blocked.
