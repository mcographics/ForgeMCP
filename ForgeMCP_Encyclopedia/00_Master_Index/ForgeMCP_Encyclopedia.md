# ForgeMCP Encyclopedia

Date: October 24, 2026
Development status: Active development; historical evidence has distinct acceptance gates.

This publication contains plugin development reports and evidence indexes. The master catalogs and relocation manifests describe a broader historical local inventory; listed local-only paths are not a promise that every artifact is published. Raw logs and recovery projects remain local. Historical titles, failed experiments, and recovery outcomes are preserved.

## Current authoritative terrain boundary

The latest [October 24 terrain acceptance audit](../14_Saved/ForgeMCP_0.2.17_Terrain_Acceptance_Verification.md) records the basin, offset lobe, and west hill saved in two scoped packages, with live ForgeTerrain MD5 `a67ab415e4c454826b8a971280b2da0e` and Original Layer MD5 `9f43572fbc2c06ef497da015879cdb23`. Live collision traces passed, but independent disk reload and close-up visual acceptance remain blocked. The west-hill job retains its historical `validation_failed_partial` result; the east hill was not applied. Earlier Stage 2E and multi-sculpt reports describe earlier baselines and do not supersede this audit.
